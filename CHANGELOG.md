# Changelog

Release notes for Musqet Merchant on Zapstore.

## [1.0.17] - 2026-10-21

- New "Send all" on Send bitcoin: fill in your whole balance in one tap, with any network fee allowed for.
- Going back from the review screen now keeps the address and amount you entered, so you can make a change without starting again.
- Small improvements to forms and loading indicators, and better crash reports on Android so we can fix problems faster.

## [1.0.16] - 2026-10-20

- New About page in the Menu: see your app version and device, copy an ID for support, and find links to My Musqet, the help centre, support, our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy, and the open-source licences the app uses.
- Amounts of one sat now read "1 sat" instead of "1 sats", in every language.
- Both wallets now use the same "Show recovery phrase" button.

## [1.0.15] - 2026-10-19

- The app can now tell you when an update is needed. If a change at Musqet ever needs a newer version, you will see a screen with a button that takes you to the update.
- While you are signed in, the app tells Musqet its version, your device model and its system version, so we can help faster if something goes wrong. Nothing else about your device is collected.
- On Android, the app is now a smaller download.
- You may be asked to accept our updated Privacy Policy.

## [1.0.14] - 2026-10-07

- A fresh look: lighter wallet cards, a sage personal wallet, and buttons that press like buttons.
- You can now replace your personal or business wallet with another one. If you have not written down the current wallet's recovery phrase, the app helps you back it up first.
- Opening the app asks for your fingerprint or face once, not twice.
- Setting up a business wallet now takes a single confirmation.
- You may be asked to accept our updated Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
